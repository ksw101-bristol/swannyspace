BLOCK STRUCTURE

📈
Wks 1–6 — Volume Phase: Build your base. Reps high (8–12), sets climb week on week. Compounds stay consistent so you can measure progress. Accessories rotate every two weeks.
🔄
Wk 7 — Deload: ~70% load, drop a set per exercise. Essential after 6 hard weeks. Gains consolidate here.
💪
Wks 8–13 — Intensity Phase: Load climbs, reps drop (5–8 on compounds). RPE 8–9. Drop sets introduced from Wk 10. Strength peaks at Wks 12–13 with 5×5.
🔄
Wk 14 — Deload: Second deload before the final push. Non-negotiable over a 17-week block.
🏆
Wks 15–17 — Peak Hypertrophy: Highest RPE of the block (9+). Drop sets on most isolation work. Everything you've built gets expressed here.

PROGRAMME PRINCIPLES

🎯
RPE: 7 = 3 reps left · 8 = 2 left · 9 = 1 left · 10 = failure. Never chase failure in volume weeks.
⏱️
Rest: Compounds 2–3 min. Isolations 60–90 sec. Intensity phase: 3–4 min on heavy compounds.
🍗
Nutrition: 0.8–1g protein/lb bodyweight. 200–300 kcal surplus throughout. Don't undereat — 17 weeks is a long block.
😴
Sleep: 7–9 hours minimum. Prioritise this especially in Wks 5–6 and 12–13.
📓
Log everything: Weight and reps for every set. You have 17 weeks of data to build — use it.

SCHEDULE

📅
Every week: Mon (Legs) · Wed (Push) · Fri (Pull) · Sat (Upper)
💡
Flex days if needed — keep at least one rest day between sessions. Mon/Wed gap is ideal; don't compress Fri/Sat into back-to-back if you can help it in the intensity phase.
