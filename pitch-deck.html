01 — The Problem
Millions play sport.
Millions can't get a game.
"The hardest part of playing sport as an adult isn't fitness. It's finding people to play with."
— The universal problem no platform has solved at scale
📱

Booking exists. Discovery doesn't.

You can book a pitch online. You can't find three strangers of your level to fill it.

⚽

Only padel got it right.

Padel's open-game matchmaking model drives its explosive growth. No other sport has replicated it.

🏟️

Facilities lose revenue daily.

Pitches and courts sit empty in off-peak slots because operators have no way to aggregate demand.

The insight: UK adults don't just want to book a court — they want to find a game. Padel's matchmaking model works because of the social layer, not the sport itself. GameOn takes that layer and opens it to every sport.
03 — The Solution
What GameOn does.
  • 1
    Open Game Listings Players post or join open slots at partner venues — no team required. Football, badminton, squash, basketball and more.
  • 2
    Dynamic Player Rating Games generate verified ratings. The more you play, the more accurately you're matched. Habit-forming by design.
  • 3
    Facility Management Layer Venues get a SaaS tool to manage bookings, pricing, and open slots — replacing legacy systems.
  • 4
    Split Payments Players pay their share in-app. Venues receive full payment guaranteed. Zero chasing.
The Loop
🏟️ Facilities list open slots
↓
🎮 Players discover & join games
↓
⭐ Ratings generated from results
↓
🔄 Players return. Slots fill faster.

08 — Why Now
The window is open.
📈
Padel proved demand at scale
UK padel open-match bookings hit 90,000/month in 2025 — without anyone targeting the broader sports market. The behaviour is proven. The TAM is untapped.
🏗️
Leisure operators need tech now
Post-COVID leisure trusts are under financial pressure. Digital tools that demonstrably increase occupancy sell themselves. Timing for facility onboarding has never been better.
📊
Infrastructure & scaling background
The founder brings a background in infrastructure rollout and scaling complex B2B networks — exactly the playbook required to solve a two-sided marketplace cold-start problem.
🔒
Moat builds fast
Verified player ratings are the moat. Once players have meaningful rating histories, they don't leave. The network effect — more players → better matches → more players — is highly defensible once running.
